Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started on SkillBridge.

Is SkillBridge free to join?
Yes — creating an account is completely free for both learners and teachers. Many courses are offered free of charge by community mentors. Premium courses and 1-on-1 mentorship sessions are priced by individual teachers and paid securely through eSewa or Khalti.
How do payments work?
When you enroll in a paid course or book a mentorship session, you pay through Nepal's most trusted digital wallets — eSewa and Khalti. Your payment is held securely until the session is completed or the course is delivered. Teachers receive their earnings after a small platform commission.
How are teachers verified?
Teachers submit credential documents (such as citizenship, educational certificates, or professional certifications) when setting up their profile. Our admin team reviews and verifies each teacher before they receive a "Verified" badge, which is displayed on their profile and course listings.
What if a session doesn't go well?
If you're unsatisfied with a mentorship session, you can submit a report through the platform's Trust & Safety system. Our team reviews reports and can issue refunds, warn teachers, or take further action depending on the situation. You can also leave an honest review to help other learners.
Can I earn a certificate?
Absolutely. When you complete all lessons and quizzes in a course, you automatically receive a verifiable PDF certificate with a unique certificate ID. You can download, print, or share it — and anyone can verify its authenticity using the certificate verification link.
How do I become a teacher on SkillBridge?
Register with a teacher account, complete your professional profile (headline, skills, hourly rate, and availability), and upload your credential documents. Once verified by our admin team, you can create courses, accept mentorship bookings, and start earning.
